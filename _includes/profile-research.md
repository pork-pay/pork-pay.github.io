### 🔊 Battery Acoustic Detection & Localization · Sep 2024–Nov 2025
Constructed the four-channel **LiB-TRAD** dataset with annotated thermal-runaway and normal-charging recordings. A BiGRU temporal fusion model achieves **over 90% recognition accuracy**, with warnings up to **101 seconds before valve opening** and **791 seconds before full thermal runaway**.

A four-microphone TDOA system with adaptive frequency-weighted GCC achieves **90.21% localization accuracy**, **4.39° mean angular error**, and approximately **0.05 m spatial accuracy**.
